YOLOv8 Baseline Error Analysis

Model:
YOLOv8n baseline model trained on the NEU-DET dataset.

Inspection:
5 validation images were inspected from each of the 6 defect classes.
Total images inspected: 30.

Classes:
Crazing
Inclusion
Patches
Pitted Surface
Rolled-in Scale
Scratches

Overall Results:
Precision: 0.6583
Recall: 0.6725
mAP@50: 0.7193
mAP@50-95: 0.3775

Observation:
Crazing and rolled-in scale showed weaker detection performance
than the other defect classes.

Patches, inclusion, pitted surface and scratches performed better.

Conclusion:
The baseline model detects all six defect categories, but
crazing and rolled-in scale need further improvement.
